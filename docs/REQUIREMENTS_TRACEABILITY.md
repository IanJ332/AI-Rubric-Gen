# CS 500 requirements traceability

Use [source register and conflict decisions](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#3-source-audit) for source IDs S1–S4 and PDF page numbers. “Request” refers to the pasted engineering instructions, with section numbers. H1–H5 refer to the Harvard sources below. PDF page numbers are one-based.

**Status.** PASS means the listed claim was verified for its stated scope. PLANNED means the design captures the requirement and its evidence is pending. BLOCKED means required source or authority was unavailable. OPEN means the team must settle the protocol detail. SUPERSEDED means the source described an earlier proposal that current Check-in #2 replaced. [Validation report](VALIDATION_REPORT.md) distinguishes executed and unexecuted checks.
